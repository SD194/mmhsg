About the community

Why we started this, and what we believe in.

Why we started this group

The idea for this community came from a simple realization: professional mental health support and peer connection are two completely different things. Therapy offers vital professional guidance, but there is a unique, irreplaceable value in connecting with people who genuinely understand the day-to-day reality of living with ADHD.We realized that many of us in and around Manipal were going through difficult experiences and navigating similar challenges entirely on our own. This group started because we wanted to change that. We wanted to create a safe, local space built on shared understanding, a community where you can just be yourself, and find the kind of peer support we all wished we had on our tougher days.

What we stand for

Safety

A space where you can be honest without fear of judgment.

Acceptance

You are welcome here exactly as you are.

Belonging

You're not an outsider. You're part of this.

Non-judgment

No labels, no assumptions, no pressure.

Local

Real people in and around Manipal, because local support matters.

Peer support, not professional care

We share lived experience. We encourage professional help when you need it.

Who this is for

This community is for anyone in and around Manipal looking for local peer support. While many of us started this journey through the lens of ADHD, we know that mental health and neurodivergence overlap in many ways. This space is for anyone who feels they could benefit from talking to people who get it. We are building a community where different minds and experiences are understood, accepted, and supported.

Where we're headed

We're starting on WhatsApp. It's simple, accessible, and where people already are.

From there, we're looking at meetups, events, discussions and other activities. The shape of the community will grow with the people in it.

You're welcome here.

You don't have to go through this alone.

Join the community

MMHSG is a peer-support community. We are not a medical or clinical service and we are not a replacement for professional therapy or medical care.

If you are in crisis or immediate danger, please contact a qualified professional or your local emergency services.
