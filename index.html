Why we started this

Why this community exists

Therapy gives us essential tools and guidance, but there is a totally different kind of relief in simply talking to someone who gets it. We started this group because navigating life when your mind works a little differently shouldn't be something you have to figure out by yourself. We wanted to build the kind of local, understanding space we always wished we had right here in Manipal,a place where you don't have to explain yourself.
