What this community can and can't offer

It's important to be honest about what MMHSG is and what it isn't.

What we can offer

  • Peer connection with people who understand what you are going through.
  • A local community in and around Manipal.
  • A space to be heard.
  • News about community events and meetups.

What we can't offer

  • Professional diagnosis or clinical assessment.
  • Therapy, counselling or medical advice.
  • Crisis intervention or emergency response.
  • A substitute for professional mental-health care.

I need help right now

If you are in crisis or immediate danger, please reach out to a qualified professional or your local emergency services. This community is not equipped to provide crisis support.

You deserve professional help, and there are people trained to support you right now. Please contact a mental-health professional, a hospital emergency department, or emergency services in your area.
