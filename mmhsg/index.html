Why we started this

Why this community exists

Through the founder's own experience with ADHD and therapy, they realised that professional mental-health support and peer connection are two very different things. Therapy gives professional guidance. But there is also real value in meeting and talking to people who personally understand what it's like to live with ADHD.

When the founder was younger and going through difficult experiences, a local community of people who understood could have helped enormously. MMHSG is the community they wish had existed, and they know there are people around Manipal who may not have an accessible local peer-support group.
